## Purpose
This document captures ideas, features, and capabilities that are explicitly out of scope for the current implementation phases but may be valuable for future iterations of the research engine.

## Ideas

### 1. Additional Platforms
- **Instagram / TikTok**: Once research access constraints are resolved, analyzing visual retrieval on social platforms.
- **Enterprise DAM Systems**: How professionals retrieve poorly remembered assets.

### 2. Longitudinal Tracking
- Tracking the same user's retrieval attempts over time to see if they learn new strategies or adapt their memory storage.

### 3. Audio/Video Context
- Integrating transcriptions of spoken video (where people talk about what they are trying to find).

### 4. Direct User Interviews
- Expanding the corpus to include structured interviews with users, rather than just public organic accounts.
